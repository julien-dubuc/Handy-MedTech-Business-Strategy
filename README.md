# Handy: MedTech Business Strategy 🦾

> **Go-to-market study for a 3D-printed bionic prosthesis concept.**

This repository gathers the strategic, financial, and market studies conducted for **Handy**, an innovative MedTech startup project. It also includes a personal strategic analysis of the automotive industry (Mercedes-Benz) as a comparative case study in business strategy.

## 🎯 Project Overview

**Handy** is a conceptual startup specializing in the design, 3D printing, and commercialization of custom myoelectric bionic prostheses for upper limbs (forearms). 

The market is currently dominated by standardized, heavy, and highly expensive prostheses (up to €60,000). Handy proposes a patient-centric alternative (€40,000, largely covered by French health insurance) focusing on:
* **Modularity:** Interchangeable hands (sports tools, hooks) with embedded tech (induction charger, phone holder).
* **Aesthetics:** Infinite personalization via 3D printing (creative partnerships like Lego/Disney) to destigmatize the device.
* **Maintenance:** Ultra-fast, decentralized after-sales service (SAV) with locally 3D-printed parts.

## 📊 Key Business Highlights (Handy)

* **Target Market:** B2B2C model. Prescribed by MPR doctors, sold to orthoprosthetists (1,300 in France), and used by patients (8,000 to 15,000 amputees in France).
* **Business Model:** Direct sales, maintenance subscriptions, and accessories.
* **Financial Projections:** 
  * Break-even expected in Year 3 with 25 prostheses sold.
  * Revenue forecast (Year 3): €875,000 HT.
* **Regulatory:** Class IIa Medical Device (Requires CE marking, ISO 13485, ISO 14971).
